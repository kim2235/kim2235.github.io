Case study

CareSuite: leading engineering for a wellness-industry mobile app

A short look at the leadership decisions behind CareSuite, a mobile app that helps spas, salons, and aesthetic clinics run their front desk.

RoleHead Developer
TeamSmall cross-functional team
Timeframe2024 – Present
StatusLive on the Apple App Store

Overview

CareSuite is a mobile app for wellness businesses such as aesthetic clinics, salons, and spas. It gives front-desk staff one place to manage schedules, recommend services and products to clients, and keep track of inventory.

The challenge

Front-desk staff juggle three jobs at once: arranging schedules, recommending the right services and products, and monitoring inventory. The goal was a single, reliable app that makes all three easier, and getting it into customers' hands on mobile.

The decisions I made

Choose the foundation for the long term

Early on, I had to pick the technical foundation for the product. I weighed two credible options against my team's skills, how well each fit where the product needed to go, and how costly it would be to change course later. I favored the option that avoided a rewrite down the road, and accepted some short-term trade-offs to get there.

Take end-to-end ownership

I took on full ownership of the product's engineering, from planning through delivery, so there is one accountable owner for quality, priorities, and releases.

Make releases safe and repeatable

I helped set up automated tests and a release process that moves every change through a test environment before it reaches production, so shipping stays low-risk.

Outcome

  • CareSuite is live on the Apple App Store.
  • I own the product's engineering end to end.
  • The foundation was chosen so the product can keep growing without starting over.

What I learned

Make foundational decisions based on where the product needs to be, not only what the first release needs. The right answer to the same question can change depending on one fact about the future.
